Larry Keeks (b. 1980, possibly near a decommissioned weather station) is a media artist and reluctant performer who claims their first exhibition happened in total darkness. They work with miscalibrated projectors, voicemail trees, and the last four payphones in a county they won't name. A rumored graduate of a school that "no longer admits time," Larry released a cassette of silence that sold out twice. Their touring show, Light Leak for One, requires audiences to face away. Collaborators describe them as "present, briefly." The archive lists more canceled works than completed ones. Moth says their next piece will be permanent. They won't say where.
